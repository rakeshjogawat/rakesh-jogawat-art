ABOUT THE ARTIST

Rakesh Jogawat

I am an Indian artist exploring the beauty of everyday life through colour, light and atmosphere.

My work spans watercolour, acrylic and oil painting, with a particular interest in landscapes, people, architecture and moments that often go unnoticed.

Each painting is created by hand and reflects my personal interpretation of the subject rather than simply reproducing what I see.

I am drawn to ordinary moments that carry a quiet sense of beauty — a landscape, a familiar face, a street, or a fleeting change of light. Through painting, I try to slow these moments down and give them a lasting form.
